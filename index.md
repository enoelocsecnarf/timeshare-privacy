# TimeShare Privacy Notice

**Effective date: 16 October 2026**

This notice describes privacy-relevant processing for the TimeShare Life features.

Health information remains on the device. TimeShare does not upload HealthKit or Health Connect data, or information derived from it, to its servers.

## Optional AI features

AI features are optional. When you explicitly choose to use one and give consent, TimeShare sends only the information needed for that request to Groq, our third-party AI provider, so Groq can generate the requested response. TimeShare does not send HealthKit or Health Connect data to Groq. TimeShare does not use AI requests or responses for advertising or tracking.

## What matters to you (Life)

*What matters to you (Life).* If you use Life, TimeShare stores what you
write there on our servers so it is available on your devices: the
things that matter to you (for example dreams, goals or directions), the
areas you group them in, the steps you add, and which of your calendar
events or series you connect to them. Only you can see them. Sharing a
calendar or inviting someone to an event does not show them any of it.
They are included in your data export and deleted with your account, and
you can delete any of them at any time.

## Progress

*Progress.* To show your streaks, Momentum and milestones, TimeShare
records on our servers the dates on which you used the app and which of
a small set of actions earned Momentum (for example finishing a step).
It is never shown to anyone else, never compared with other people, and
cannot be bought, sold or exchanged. It is included in your data export
and deleted with your account.

## Monthly Compass

*Monthly Compass.* When a month ends, TimeShare stores a short summary of
it on our servers: scores for six dimensions, how confident they are, and
the counts behind them (for example, how many days you rated or planned).
It contains no event titles, notes, individual ratings, health data or
AI-written text. Only you can see it. You can delete one month or all of
them in Settings, it is included in your data export, and it is deleted
with your account. If you delete a month's mood ratings, the mood part of
that month's summary is deleted too. A month you delete is not created
again unless you ask for it to be recalculated; to honour that, we keep a
note of which months you deleted (the month only), which is deleted with
your account.

## Sharing something that matters

*Sharing something that matters.* You can choose to share one of the
things that matter to you with people you already plan with in
TimeShare. They see only that one item — its title, description, status,
target date, steps and the people it is shared with — and, if you allow
it, can add and update its steps. They never see your other goals, your
areas, your calendar, your mood, your progress or your Monthly Compass.
Nothing is shared until you share it, and nothing is visible to them
until they accept. You can remove someone, or they can leave, at any
time; they then see nothing of it any more. Steps someone else adds
remain part of your item.

## General

These features are designed so that information is private by default and is shared only where the user explicitly chooses to share it.

This notice will be expanded as the public application release progresses.
